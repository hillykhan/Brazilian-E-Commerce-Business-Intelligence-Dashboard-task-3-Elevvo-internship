# Brazilian E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard
(Elevvo Data Analytics Internship Project – Hilal Khan)

# 🔹 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business intelligence analysis of the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist). The objective was to perform end-to-end data analysis including data cleaning, transformation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and interactive dashboard development using Power BI to generate actionable business insights.

The analysis covers revenue performance, customer behavior, product trends, shipping performance, and customer satisfaction.

## 🔹 Key Performance Metrics

* **Total Revenue:** $15.84M
* **Total Orders:** 99K
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $159.33
* **Average Delivery Time:** 43.1 days
* **Average Review Rating:** 4.09 / 5

# 🔹 Key Business Insights

# 1️⃣ Revenue Growth

Revenue increased significantly from 2016 to 2018, indicating strong business expansion and increasing market demand.

# 2️⃣ Product Performance

A small number of product categories contribute the majority of total revenue, highlighting revenue concentration within top-performing categories.

# 3️⃣ Shipping Impact

Standard shipping mode generates the highest revenue contribution compared to other shipping methods.

# 4️⃣ Delivery & Customer Satisfaction

Customer review ratings are influenced by delivery time. Longer delivery durations show slight negative impact on satisfaction levels.

# 5️⃣ Geographic Contribution

Certain seller cities dominate revenue generation, indicating regional performance disparities.

# 🔹 Strategic Recommendations

✔ Optimize logistics operations to reduce delivery time and improve customer ratings.
✔ Focus marketing investment on high-performing product categories.
✔ Expand successful product lines in high-revenue cities.
✔ Implement retention strategies for high-value customers.
✔ Analyze underperforming regions to identify growth opportunities.

# 🔹 Conclusion

The dashboard delivers actionable insights that support strategic decision-making in revenue optimization, logistics efficiency, and customer experience improvement.

This project demonstrates practical application of data modeling, DAX calculations, data visualization, and business storytelling using Power BI.
